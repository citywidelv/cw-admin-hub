How it flows

Received came in (an upload, a form, a PandaDoc you saw). Verified someone checked it and it is right. Needs a fix it came in wrong, with a note saying what. A vendor is ready to activate when every required item is Verified and the crew has cleared.

Feeds that fill items on their own: the Vendor Evaluation, uploads of COIs, the notarized waiver and the bank letter, and background check requests. PandaDoc packets and CRM steps are by hand.

Import from Asana

Reads the four onboarding boards and the IC Background Checks board with the Asana token on the script. Each Asana task becomes a record here, checked-off subtasks become Verified items, open task comments become notes, and each background check request becomes a request plus a Pending person on the roster. Safe to run more than once: records are keyed on the Asana task, and nothing edited here is overwritten by Asana. Asana itself is never changed.
