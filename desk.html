Why one page beats one row

A row is 30 to 60 cells wide. You scroll sideways, lose the row you were on, and read headers like pay_period. A page shows the same record with plain labels, grouped the way you think about it, and nothing else in view.

Safer than the Sheet

Only the fields you change are written. If someone edited a cell on the Sheet after you opened the record, that field is skipped and you are told. Every change is logged with who, when and the previous value, with Undo.

When to open the Sheet

Bulk changes, pricing tabs, or a column this desk does not show. Every list has an Open the Sheet button and every record has a This row in the Sheet link that lands on the exact row.
